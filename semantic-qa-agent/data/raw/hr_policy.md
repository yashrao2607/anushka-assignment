# HR Policy Handbook 2026

## 1. Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year, accrued at 2 days per completed month of service. Leave must be applied
for through the HR portal at least five working days in advance, except in the
case of medical emergencies.

Interns and fixed-term contractors accrue casual leave at the rate of 0.5 days
per completed month, to a maximum of 6 days per year. Unused casual leave does
not carry over into the following year and is not encashable on exit.

## 2. Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate
is required for any absence exceeding two consecutive working days. Sick leave
is separate from annual leave and does not reduce the annual entitlement.

## 3. Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks. Notice of at least eight weeks should be
given to the reporting manager and to HR where circumstances permit.

## 4. Working Hours and Remote Work

Standard working hours are 09:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour
break. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their
manager's approval. Fully remote arrangements require director-level sign-off
and are reviewed every six months.
