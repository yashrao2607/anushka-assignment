IT SUPPORT FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

DEVICE FAILS TO BOOT
If a device shows no display and no power indicator, first disconnect all
peripherals and hold the power button for fifteen seconds. Reconnect the
charger and wait two minutes before attempting to power on again. If the
machine still does not start, raise a ticket with error code ERR_4092 and the
hardware team will arrange a replacement within one business day.

PASSWORD RESET
Passwords expire every 90 days. A self-service reset is available at the
identity portal. Three failed attempts will lock the account for 30 minutes.
Accounts locked for a fourth time must be unlocked by the service desk.

VPN CONNECTION ISSUES
If the VPN client reports error code ERR_7731, the certificate on the device has
expired. Reinstalling the client from the software centre reissues the
certificate automatically. VPN access is required for all internal systems when
working outside the office network.

SOFTWARE INSTALLATION REQUESTS
Software not present in the software centre requires a security review before
installation. Submit the request with a business justification. Standard review
time is three business days.
