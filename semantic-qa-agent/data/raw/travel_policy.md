# Travel and Expense Guidelines

## 4.1 Booking Procedure

All business travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel desk.
Bookings made outside this channel require written pre-approval from a director
and may be rejected at the reimbursement stage.

## 4.2 Non-refundable Bookings

Where a booking is non-refundable and the trip is cancelled due to a change on
the client side, the cost remains reimbursable provided that the employee
obtains manager approval and submits the claim within 30 days of the original
travel date. Cancellations arising from personal reasons are not reimbursable.

## 4.3 Daily Allowance

The daily allowance for domestic travel is INR 2,500 per day, covering meals and
local transport. For international travel the allowance is USD 75 per day.
Receipts are not required for the daily allowance but are required for all
lodging and airfare claims.

## 4.4 Submitting a Claim

Expense claims are submitted through the finance portal with scanned receipts
attached. Claims older than 60 days will not be processed. Reimbursements are
settled with the next payroll cycle following approval.
