# New Joiner Onboarding FAQ

## Before Your First Day

Your offer letter, background verification form and bank details must be
submitted at least five working days before the joining date. Delays in
verification are the most common cause of a delayed system account.

## First Week

Your laptop and access badge are issued on day one at the facilities desk.
System accounts are typically active within four hours of your joining
formalities being completed. If your account is not active by the end of day
one, contact the service desk with your employee ID.

## Probation

The standard probation period is six months. A formal review is held at the
three-month and six-month marks. Confirmation of employment is communicated in
writing by HR following the six-month review.

## Buddy Programme

Every new joiner is assigned a buddy from their team for the first 60 days. The
buddy is a peer, not a manager, and is there for informal questions about tools,
process and team norms.
